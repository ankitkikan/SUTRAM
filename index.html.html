Our Story

Rooted in India's Soul

"We do not merely sell objects — we deliver intention, energy and craft."

Sutram is a New Delhi based artisan brand founded on a singular conviction: that the healing power of natural crystals and the timeless beauty of Indian craft deserve a global home.

We source our crystals with discernment from across India and internationally — selecting only specimens of genuine quality, authentic energy, and natural character. Each piece is chosen not merely for its appearance, but for the story it carries within.

From copper wire jewelry crafted by skilled artisans to hand-painted wooden clocks and centuries-old textile traditions — everything that bears the Sutram name has been chosen with care and delivered with pride to homes across the United Kingdom, United States, Australia, and beyond.
